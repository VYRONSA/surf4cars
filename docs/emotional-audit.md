# Emotional audit — Rule 2

The intended emotion of every public page, and whether the page currently delivers it within three
seconds. Judged from the screenshot pack, not from the code.

| Page | Intended emotion | Delivered in 3s? | What carries it — or what stops it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Homepage** | Excitement — *"I need to see more."* | **Yes** | Chapman's Peak at blue hour, full bleed, one instruction, one search. The strongest surface on the platform. |
| **Marketplace** | Discovery — *"There are amazing cars here."* | **Partly** | The layout delivers it: three photographs across, no chrome, a headline that names the collection. The photography undercuts it — the first row currently shows a motor-show hall and a workshop. Structure passes, content does not. |
| **Vehicle detail** | Ownership — *"I can picture this in my driveway."* | **Partly** | The full-bleed hero with the name and price set into the frame is exactly right. Same limitation: the frame is a library photograph of an industrial yard rather than of this car. |
| **Dealer profile** | Trust — *"I would buy from these people."* | **Yes** | Named honestly, gaps declared rather than filled, stock shown at size. The "not provided" fields do more for trust than invented ones would. |
| **Sign in** | Confidence — *"I am entering a premium platform."* | **Yes** | Since the shell rebuild: photography on one side, a 27rem form on the other, the wordmark present. |
| **Create account** | Opportunity — *"I want to be part of this."* | **Yes** | Two plainly-worded choices — "I'm looking for a car" / "I sell cars" — instead of two boxes labelled BUYER and DEALER. |
| **Buyer sign-up** | Opportunity | **Yes** | — |
| **Dealer sign-up** | Ambition — *"This is worth ten minutes."* | **Yes** | Says what it will take and what to have ready, rather than promising the future of dealership growth. |
| **Forgot password** | Reassurance — *"This will be sorted."* | **Yes** | One field, one action, a way back. |
| **Not found** | Redirection — *"Fine, take me somewhere good."* | **Yes** | Names the likely cause (the car sold) and offers the marketplace. |
| **Access denied** | Clarity without accusation | **Yes** | Says which part of the platform this is, not "403 Access denied". |

## The pattern

**Nine of eleven pages deliver their emotion. The two that only partly do are the two that matter
most commercially — the marketplace and the vehicle page — and both fail for the same reason.**

It is not layout, hierarchy, typography or spacing. Those were rebuilt and they work. It is that a
buyer looking at a car photographed in a motor-show hall cannot picture it in their driveway, and no
amount of framing fixes a photograph of the wrong setting.

This is the same finding as the photography audit, arrived at from the opposite direction. The
interface is ahead of the imagery, and the gap is now visible in the one measure the Founder set:
emotion.

## What would close it

Twelve photographs. One hero and eleven cars spanning the body styles the diversity pass already
selects for. Both partial pages become full passes, and the marketplace's first row stops being the
weakest thing a visitor sees.
